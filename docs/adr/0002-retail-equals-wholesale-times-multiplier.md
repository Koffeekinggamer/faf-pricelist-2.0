# Retail = wholesale (cost) × multiplier

Floor-facing price shown is always **wholesale (cost) × per-vendor multiplier**, then rolled up to the next **even whole dollar** (exact even dollars stay put). Default multiplier is **2.7**; **Genuine Oak** is **1.7**. Saved vendor mults beat workbook markup unless the importer explicitly opts into markup. Search shows RETAIL; imports store `price_basis = wholesale`. Undermount Drawer Slides retail at wholesale (no markup).
